Event Rules & Payment Policy

Terms & Conditions

Effective Date: October 2026 • Organized by Angamaly Sports Association & Jeevadhara Renal Care Foundation

1. General Agreement

By accessing this website and registering for Angamaly Marathon 2027 (3rd Edition), scheduled for Sunday, 17 January 2027 at De Paul Institute of Science & Technology (DiST), Angamaly, Ernakulam, Kerala, you agree to be bound by these Terms & Conditions. The event is jointly organized by Jeevadhara Renal Care Foundation and Angamaly Sports Association ("Organizers").

2. Race Categories & Registration Fees

All registration fees are quoted and payable in Indian Rupees (INR ₹) and include race participation, official Bib number, participant T-shirt (subject to selected size availability), finisher medal, and post-run refreshments:

Race Category Early Bird Fee Regular Fee
Half Marathon – 21.1KM (Competitive) ₹700 ₹750
Mini Marathon – 10KM (Competitive) ₹550 ₹600
Fun Run / Walk – 5KM (Family Friendly) ₹300 ₹350
Group of Students (Min 5 Students) ₹200 per student (Minimum 5 students = ₹1,000; valid Student ID required)

Early Bird rates apply strictly until the published Early Bird deadline on the website, after which Regular registration fees automatically apply.

3. Online Payments & Payment Gateway Terms

Payments for event registration are collected online in favour of Jeevadhara Renal Care Foundation through RBI-authorized third-party Payment Gateway aggregators and official banking channels (supporting UPI, Debit Cards, Credit Cards, NetBanking, Wallets, and Verified Merchant QR).

  • By initiating a payment on our website, you authorize our Payment Gateway partner to charge the applicable registration amount in INR (₹).
  • Your registration is confirmed once payment settlement/verification is completed and an official Registration Reference Code and Bib Number are generated.
  • Users must not use unauthorized payment instruments or submit forged/altered payment receipts. Any fraudulent payment submission will result in immediate cancellation of registration without refund.

4. Refund, Cancellation & Transfer Policy

Standard Registration Cancellation & Refund Policy:
  • No Cancellations or Refunds: Once a registration is completed and payment is confirmed, the registration fee is non-refundable under normal circumstances, as funds are immediately allocated toward race logistics, customized T-shirt production, timing bibs, and charitable renal care support.
  • Non-Transferable: Confirmed race entries and Bib numbers cannot be transferred or resold to another person without explicit prior written approval from the Organizers.
  • Duplicate / Excess Payment Refund: If a participant is accidentally charged twice for the same registration due to a Payment Gateway timeout, network interruption, or bank error, the duplicate transaction amount will be refunded 100% to the original mode of payment within 5 to 7 working days upon verification of transaction details.
  • Event Rescheduling / Force Majeure: In the event of severe weather, natural calamity, government orders, or unforeseen public safety directives requiring the marathon to be rescheduled to a new date, all existing registrations will automatically remain valid for the rescheduled date.

5. Eligibility, Age Rules & Student Group Verification

  • Participants must enter their accurate Date of Birth as per official government ID. Age is calculated as on 17 January 2027.
  • Participants below 18 years of age must participate with the consent and supervision of a parent, legal guardian, or school/college faculty coordinator.
  • For Group of Students registrations (₹200 per student, minimum 5 students), the Team Leader must upload a valid Student ID card during online registration, and all accompanying students must present their physical school/college ID cards during Bib & Kit collection on Saturday, 16 January 2027.

6. Bib & Race Kit Collection

  • Bib numbers and T-shirts must be collected in person at De Paul Institute of Science & Technology (DiST), Angamaly on Saturday, 16 January 2027 between 10:00 AM and 6:00 PM.
  • Participants must present their confirmation email/slip along with a valid photo ID (and Student IDs for student group members) at the distribution counter.
  • The official race Bib must be pinned visibly on the front of the runner's chest throughout the race.

7. Medical Fitness, Assumption of Risk & Waiver of Liability

Long-distance running and road racing involve inherent physical exertion and risks. By registering for Angamaly Marathon 2027, each participant declares and warrants that:

  • They are medically fit and sufficiently trained to participate in their chosen distance category (21.1KM, 10KM, or 5KM).
  • They voluntarily assume full responsibility for any injury, illness, medical condition, or personal property loss occurring before, during, or after the event.
  • They consent to receive first-aid and emergency medical treatment provided by the event's medical team if required on race day.
  • They release and hold harmless Jeevadhara Renal Care Foundation, Angamaly Sports Association, De Paul Institute of Science & Technology (DiST), event sponsors, volunteers, and race officials from any liability or claims arising from their participation, to the fullest extent permitted under Indian law.

8. Race Conduct & Decisions of Officials

Participants must follow the designated race route, safety marshals' instructions, traffic police directions, and cut-off timings. Any unsportsmanlike conduct, route shortcutting, or Bib swapping will lead to immediate disqualification. The decision of the Race Director and Technical Committee regarding race results, age categories, and cash prizes shall be final and binding.

9. Governing Law & Jurisdiction

These Terms & Conditions are governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India. Any disputes arising in connection with the event or website transactions shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in Ernakulam District, Kerala.
