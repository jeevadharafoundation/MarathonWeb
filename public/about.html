About the Movement

About Angamaly Marathon

Running for Health. Racing for Hope.

Angamaly Marathon is more than a sporting event; it is a movement that brings people together for fitness, compassion, and social change.

Angamaly Marathon Community
Edition 3 • 17 January 2027 • DiST, Angamaly
Organizers & Origins

Rooted in Compassion and Community Well-Being

Organised jointly by Angamaly Sports Association and Jeevadhara Foundation to unite civic spirit with meaningful social impact.

Core Mission

Angamaly Sports Association & Jeevadhara Foundation

Organised by Angamaly Sports Association and Jeevadhara Foundation, the marathon began with a mission to support kidney and dialysis patients while promoting health awareness and community well-being.

Bringing athletes, local leaders, medical professionals, and families onto one shared track of solidarity.

Healthcare & Welfare • Est. 2012

Jeevadhara Foundation

Jeevadhara Foundation, established in 2012, has been actively involved in healthcare assistance, social welfare, and supporting economically disadvantaged patients.

All marathon proceeds directly reinforce compassionate healthcare outreach and critical patient aid initiatives.

Social Impact

Purpose Behind Every Stride

Through every step, we aim to raise awareness about drug abuse, inspire healthier lifestyles, and extend hope and support to those battling kidney-related illnesses.

Kidney & Dialysis Support

Providing direct financial and medical aid to underprivileged kidney and dialysis patients, easing treatment burdens and offering dignified care.

Substance-Free Living

Empowering youth and community members to embrace physical fitness, mental resilience, and the life-affirming choice of "Miles, Not Drugs."

Community Health Awareness

Promoting preventive healthcare, cardiovascular wellness, and active lifestyles across all generations throughout Angamaly and Kerala.

Our Commitment
One Marathon. One Community. One Mission.
“Together, let's run towards a healthier, stronger, and drug-free tomorrow.”
